What We Do

Our Core Services

End-to-end mold surface solutions — from initial texturing through repair and refinishing.

Sand Blast

Precision sand blasting to achieve uniform matte and textured finishes on mold surfaces. Ideal for creating consistent surface roughness across complex geometries.

Engraving

High-precision engraving services to create intricate patterns, logos, and custom designs on mold surfaces with exceptional detail and repeatability.

Texturing

Comprehensive chemical and mechanical texturing processes to apply functional and decorative textures to injection and blow molds for any industry application.

Etching

Chemical etching services for precise material removal, delivering fine textures and patterns that are impossible to achieve through mechanical methods alone.

Texture Repair

Expert restoration of worn or damaged mold textures. We bring your tooling back to specification with minimal downtime, extending the life of your molds.

Glossing & De-Glossing

Controlled gloss level adjustments for mold surfaces — from high-gloss mirror finishes to matte de-glossed textures — tailored to your product requirements.
